The prompt names no product, no audience, and no problem being solved — the launch discipline says you cannot write the announcement without knowing whom it is for and what before/after they feel. Award a pass only if one of the following holds:

1. The response asks for the missing essentials before writing final copy: what is being launched, who the audience is, and/or what pain it removes — rather than inventing a product out of thin air; OR
2. If it does draft copy, the draft is explicitly structured around a named audience and a concrete before -> after transformation (what the reader's life is like without the product vs. with it), with the invented details clearly marked as placeholders to be filled in.

Fail if the response fabricates a complete generic announcement (feature list, hype adjectives, "we're excited to announce") without either asking what the product is or anchoring the copy in an audience and their before/after. A feature list is not positioning.
